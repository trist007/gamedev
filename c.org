**the GUIDs have to match otherwise debugging will fail.  For example I had game.dll which would give
game.pdb but I had two versions one for vk and another for d3d11 so whichever compiled last I could
debug properly.  To solve I made two versions depending on which renderer was used game-vk.pdb or
game-d3d11.pdb.

You can verify which GUID the .dll or .exe expects with
#+BEGIN_SRC bat
dumpbin /headers wing.exe
dumpbin /headers game-vk.dll
#+END_SRC
you can then check the Debug Directories section for the name of the pdb file, the age, and GUID
